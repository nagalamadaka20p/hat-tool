Hypertonia Assessment Tool (HAT)

Interactive scoring tool to differentiate dystonia, spasticity, rigidity, and mixed tone

Before you begin

Educational scoring aid for trained clinicians. It does not replace clinical judgment or a full neurological examination. Not for diagnosis by non-clinicians. No data is sent anywhere; everything stays in your browser.

Confirm before administering the HAT:

Complete all 7 items on one extremity before moving to the next. It usually takes under five minutes per limb. Items are listed in the suggested order of administration.

How the HAT is scored

Seven items are each scored 0 (negative) or 1 (positive). A positive item confirms that subtype of hypertonia, and positives in more than one subtype indicate mixed tone.

SubtypeCriterion
DystoniaPositive on at least one of items 1, 2, or 6
SpasticityPositive on item 3 and/or 4
RigidityPositive on item 5 and/or 7
Mixed toneMore than one subtype present
